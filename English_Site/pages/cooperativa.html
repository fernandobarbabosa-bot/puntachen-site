Our cooperative

A fishermen's cooperative driving local development.

Since 2008, we have worked to make responsible use of the sea, protect our environment, and strengthen the local economy for our families and neighbors.

Tradition with a vision

More than a decade of shared work, combining fishing expertise with responsible practices to build a sustainable project.

Local economy

Our cooperative supports local employment and better working conditions for fishermen in the region, creating a meaningful social impact.
